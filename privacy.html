1. Who we are

Sora Digital FZE is a performance media buying company registered in the Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, Sharjah, United Arab Emirates. We run paid user-acquisition campaigns for apps and digital products on platforms such as Meta, TikTok, Google and Snapchat. For the purposes of this policy, Sora Digital FZE is the controller of the personal data described here.

2. Data we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you email us or message us on WhatsApp or Telegram, such as your name, company, job title, email address, phone number and messaging handle.
  • Business information you share about your offer, target geos, budgets, payouts and campaign performance.
  • Billing details for clients, such as company registration, billing address, tax identifiers and payment records.
  • Technical data that is automatically sent by your browser when you load our website, such as IP address, browser type and device information. This is processed by our hosting provider and by Google Fonts, which serves the site’s typefaces.
  • Campaign data that we access in advertising and analytics accounts while delivering services. This is usually aggregated reporting data. Where it includes end-user personal data, we process it on our client’s behalf and under their instructions.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and assess whether we’re a fit for your campaign.
  • To plan, run, optimise and report on advertising campaigns.
  • To issue invoices, collect payments and keep financial records.
  • To keep our website and systems secure and working properly.
  • To meet legal, tax, regulatory and advertising-platform obligations.
  • To send occasional business updates, which you can opt out of at any time.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Legal bases

We process personal data where it is needed to enter into or perform a contract with you, where we have a legitimate business interest (such as answering enquiries and improving our services), where we must comply with a legal obligation, or with your consent. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Sharing

We share personal data only as needed with:

  • Advertising platforms (Meta, TikTok, Google, Snapchat) when we set up and manage campaigns.
  • Service providers that support our business, such as hosting, email, messaging, accounting and payment providers.
  • Professional advisers, such as lawyers, auditors and accountants.
  • Authorities, regulators or courts where required by law.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is reorganised, merged or sold.

6. International transfers

We work with clients and partners in more than 26 countries, so personal data may be transferred outside the UAE or your home country. When we transfer data, we take reasonable steps to make sure it stays protected, including using contractual safeguards where required.

7. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes above. Enquiry data that doesn’t lead to a working relationship is normally deleted within 24 months. Client and financial records are kept for as long as UAE law and tax rules require.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls on advertising accounts and limiting access to staff who need it. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Ask us to delete your data.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent and opt out of marketing.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on the request.

10. Cookies

Our website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. If we add analytics or tracking tools in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

11. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its effective date.
